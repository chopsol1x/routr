How it works

Set the split once. Every payment follows it.

routr.fun watches the wallet your revenue lands in and pays it out by your rules. Funds move from your wallet to your destinations, never through ours.

1

Revenue arrives

Fees, sales and royalties land in your routing wallet as USDC or SOL. Each payment is recorded once, so it can never be paid out twice.

2

Your rules split it

Pick destinations and percentages. The plan has to total exactly 100% after fees before it can go live, and you see every amount before it moves.

3

You approve, it pays out

Pay out instantly, on a schedule, or once a minimum builds up. Every transfer is signed, confirmed on Solana and logged with its signature.

    09

    Security

    Noncustodial by design.

    We coordinate payouts. We never hold your funds or your keys.

    No seed phrases, ever

    We never ask for or store a seed phrase or private key. You connect through a standard Solana wallet.

    You sign what moves

    Plans, recipients and automation limits need your signature. Anything outside them asks again.

    No double payouts

    Every revenue event has a unique key. Retries check the chain first, so a payment is never sent twice.

    Full audit trail

    Rule changes, approvals, payouts and failures are all recorded and exportable.

    Honest statuses

    Queued, submitted and confirmed are different things, and we show them as different things.

    Demo stays demo

    Preview mode is clearly marked and can't send transactions. Live mode always says so.
